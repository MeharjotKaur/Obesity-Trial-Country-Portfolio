# Decision Charter — Draft pending T0000

## Client archetype

A mid-sized biopharma sponsor preparing a multinational Phase III programme for an oral obesity therapy in adults without diabetes.

## Decision

Select a robust five-country portfolio for preliminary clinical-development planning.

## Candidate decision criteria

1. Addressable adult population with obesity
2. Relevant active-trial saturation
3. Historical obesity-trial site experience
4. Healthcare and research readiness
5. Geographic diversification
6. Public-data confidence

## Explicit non-claims

- The tool does not select individual investigators or sites.
- It does not estimate actual country recruitment rates.
- It does not estimate country trial costs.
- It does not replace regulatory, feasibility, or primary-research work.

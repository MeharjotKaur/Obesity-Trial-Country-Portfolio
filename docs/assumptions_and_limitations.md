# Assumptions and limitations

## What is sourced

- Registered study attributes and locations.
- WHO adult obesity prevalence.
- World Bank population and regional reference.

## What is derived

- Relevance cohort from documented therapeutic-intent rules.
- Country-level counts, ratios and proxy variables.
- Normalised pillar and attractiveness scores.
- Optimised portfolios and selection frequencies.

## What is assumed

- Country screen thresholds.
- Pillar weights and component weights.
- Portfolio diversity, capacity and confidence constraints.
- Monte Carlo distribution and threshold-jitter range.

## Material limitations

1. Registered locations represent historical evidence, not confirmed future capacity or investigator availability.
2. Study enrollment is not allocated to countries; no country recruitment-rate claim is made.
3. The obesity-burden value is a population proxy, not an eligible-patient forecast.
4. Active registered trials are an incomplete competition proxy and not commercial intelligence.
5. Scores are relative to the screened candidate set and depend on transformations and weights.
6. Public sources omit sponsor-specific cost, regulatory, supply, contracting and policy constraints.
7. Country-level analysis masks within-country geographic and demographic variation.
8. Russia appears only as an assumption-sensitive alternative and requires explicit operational and geopolitical diligence.
9. Monte Carlo varies decision assumptions, not all source-estimate uncertainty.

## Appropriate claim

"Built a reproducible decision-support model that screens countries and identifies a robust preliminary portfolio under stated assumptions."

## Inappropriate claim

"Predicted the five countries that will recruit fastest or make the trial succeed."
